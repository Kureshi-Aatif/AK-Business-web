When customers in Surat need an immediate service or want to visit a store, over 80% search on Google Maps. If your listing is missing, poorly configured, or lacks customer reviews, you are handing valuable local customers directly to your competitors.

Learning **how to set up a Google Business Profile** (formerly Google My Business) correctly is the single most cost-effective marketing step any local business owner can take.

In this practical guide, we walk you through the exact setup steps and optimization secrets we use at AK Digital Studio to help clients rank in Surat.

---

## Step 1: Claim and Verify Your Business

1. Visit [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in with your official business Google Account.
2. Enter your exact business name.  
   *Important rule*: Do not spam your business title with unnecessary keywords (e.g. use *"AK Digital Studio"*, not *"AK Digital Studio Best Web Designer Surat Cheap"*). Google’s spam filters will suspend profiles with keyword-stuffed titles.
3. Choose whether you have a physical storefront where customers visit or a service-area business (where you travel to clients).
4. Enter your exact address in Surat (including pincode) and complete the verification process via video recording or SMS code.

---

## Step 2: Choose the Right Primary Category

Your primary category is the single heaviest ranking factor in Google's local algorithm:
- Choose the category that best describes your core business (e.g., *Website Designer*, *Textile Merchant*, *Jewelry Designer*, *Marketing Agency*).
- Add 3 to 5 relevant secondary categories.

Do not guess categories—search your top-ranking local competitors in Surat on Google Maps and note the category displayed under their business name.

---

## Step 3: Connect Your Official Website (NAP Consistency)

Google constantly verifies the legitimacy of your profile by cross-referencing your Name, Address, and Phone number (**NAP**) with your website.

- In your profile's website field, provide your canonical HTTPS URL (e.g., `https://akdigitalstudio.com`).
- Add UTM tracking parameters so you can measure how many website visitors come from Google Maps in Google Analytics:  
  `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_listing`
- Ensure the phone number on your listing matches the phone number listed on your website's contact page and footer.

---

## Step 4: Populate Products, Services, and High-Resolution Photos

Incomplete profiles rarely rank in the top 3 spots. Make sure to:
- **Add Products**: Include your core product lines or service packages with starting prices and direct links to your site.
- **Upload Real Photos**: Upload pictures of your office, store front, team, and recent client work. Avoid generic stock photos; Google prioritizes original, geo-tagged mobile photos.
- **Set Accurate Hours**: Keep your opening hours accurate, including festival holidays (Diwali, Uttarayan).

---

## Step 5: Build a System for Genuine Google Reviews

Google reviews directly influence both your ranking position and customer conversion rates:
1. Generate your short review link from your GBP dashboard.
2. Share this link on WhatsApp with happy clients immediately upon project completion.
3. Encourage clients to mention the specific service and location in their review.
4. Reply to every review promptly and professionally.
