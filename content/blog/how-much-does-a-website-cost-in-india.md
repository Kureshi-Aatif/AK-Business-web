## 2. Mandatory Ongoing Costs: Domain & Web Hosting

In addition to design and development, every live website requires two technical assets:

1. **Domain Name (.com or .in)**:
   - Typical cost: **₹700 to ₹1,200 per year**.
   - A `.in` domain is ideal for targeting Indian domestic buyers, while `.com` is standard for global reach.
2. **Cloud Web Hosting**:
   - Typical cost: **₹2,000 to ₹4,500 per year** (on reliable cloud providers such as Hostinger, MilesWeb, or AWS Lightsail).
   - *Tip*: Avoid ultra-cheap ₹50/month shared hosts. Slow hosting hurts your Google rankings and increases mobile visitor bounce rates.
