When entrepreneurs and shop owners in India decide to sell their products online, they almost immediately face the classic dilemma: **Shopify vs WordPress (WooCommerce) — which platform is better for my small business?**

Both platforms power millions of digital stores worldwide, but they have drastically different cost structures, technical demands, and customization flexibilities.

In this clear, unbiased comparison, we evaluate both platforms across initial costs, monthly recurring fees, ease of use, payment gateway support in India, and overall scalability.

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Monthly Subscription** | ₹1,999 to ₹7,499+/month | ₹0 (Platform is free) | ₹0 (No monthly fees) |

## 1. Cost & Recurring Fees: The Hidden Shopify Penalty

The biggest surprise for small businesses starting on Shopify in India is recurring operational expenses:
- **Monthly Subscription**: Even the basic Shopify plan costs roughly **₹2,000 every single month**, regardless of whether you make any sales that month. Over 3 years, that is **₹72,000+** in platform rent alone.
- **Paid Apps**: Many basic features (such as custom WhatsApp order notifications or Indian pincode delivery estimators) require third-party monthly paid apps that can quickly add another ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 each month.
- **Transaction Charges**: Shopify levies an additional transaction fee if you use external payment processors.

On **WordPress (WooCommerce)**, the software is completely free and open-source. You only pay for your annual hosting (approx. ₹3,000/year) and domain name, making it significantly more affordable for long-term Indian operations.

---

## 2. Ease of Use & Technical Setup

- **Shopify**: Exceptional user experience. Everything is hosted in the cloud, security certificates are automatic, and inventory management is straightforward. Ideal for non-technical store owners who have healthy monthly marketing budgets.
- **WordPress**: Offers infinite customization and complete ownership of your database. However, WordPress sites require routine plugin updates, security monitoring, and caching configuration to avoid becoming slow.

## Conclusion & Recommendation

- **Choose Shopify** if you have an established product line, venture capital or steady monthly revenue, and want an out-of-the-box solution where you do not mind paying ₹25,000+ per year in recurring subscriptions.
- **Choose WordPress / WooCommerce** if you want full code ownership, deep SEO control, and lower long-term costs.
- **Choose a Custom Built Catalogue or Store** if you want blazing speed, zero monthly fees, and direct WhatsApp buyer engagement.
