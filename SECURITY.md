# Security Policy & Vulnerability Disclosure

AK Digital Studio takes the security and privacy of our web applications and client data seriously. We welcome responsible disclosure of potential security vulnerabilities.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in our website or digital infrastructure, please notify us immediately by emailing:

- **Security Contact**: `AKdigitalstudio.org@gmail.com`
- **Lead Developer**: Aatif Kureshi (`+91 76984 50003`)
- **Direct Location**: Surat, Gujarat, India

Please include:
1. A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
2. Step-by-step reproduction steps or a non-destructive Proof of Concept (PoC).
3. Any affected URLs, request parameters, or headers.

---

## Guidelines for Responsible Research
- Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate proof-of-concept.
- Do not access, modify, delete, or exfiltrate private client inquiries, phone numbers, or database records.
- Do not perform Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, spam automated submission endpoints, or degrade server availability.
- Allow us a reasonable timeframe (typically 7–14 business days) to resolve the vulnerability before any public disclosure.
