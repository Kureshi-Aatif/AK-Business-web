## 3. Systematic Review Collection Playbook

Positive, keyword-rich Google Reviews with photos represent over 15% of Google Maps local 3-pack ranking signals.

### The 4-Step Review Generation Workflow:
1. **Timing**: Request a review within 48 hours of successful project deployment or milestone approval (when client excitement is at its highest).
2. **Review Link Shortcut**:
   - Generate your short review link in Google Business Profile (`https://g.page/r/[your-id]/review`).
   - Add this link into `.env` as `GOOGLE_REVIEW_URL` so the website's review CTA button dynamically activates.
3. **Pre-Written WhatsApp Template for Clients**:
   > *"Hi [Client Name], it was a pleasure building your website [Website Name]! If you loved working with us, could you take 60 seconds to share your experience on our Google profile? A short note mentioning the website speed and how we supported your business in Surat helps us tremendously:*  
   > *⭐ [Direct Google Review Link]*  
   > *Thank you so much for trusting AK Digital Studio!"*
4. **Owner Review Response Protocol**:
   - Respond to every review within 24 hours.
   - Mention the specific service and city naturally (e.g., *"Thank you [Name]! We loved designing the custom textile catalogue website for your business here in Surat. Wishing you continuous digital growth!"*).
